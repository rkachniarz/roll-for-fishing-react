decisions and requirements behind it, its tests, and where else to read. Start here before changing something
next to what exists: the decisions listed are every one its files cite or that names them, oldest first.

**Not here:** the reasons themselves (`docs/decisions.md`), sources (`docs/sources.md`), plans (`DEV_PLAN.md`).
Which files make an area is kept in `tools/areas.mjs`; a test fails if a file is in none.

**Traps** (each file's `Trap:` lines; the file has the detail):

- `src/engine/cast.js`: `tickCast` checks for a late strike straight after the bite starts, in the same tick.
- `src/engine/features.js`: A big fish is a pool fish marked `of` its feature's `born` (D156), brought in by `bringFeature` and taken out by `partFish` when the feature goes or the player travels; the pool's turns, its room and a random bite leave it alone.
- `src/engine/features.js`: "Predator" means two things: a surface feature's predator prefers predator bait (D102); the tank and hiding use the fish's `predator` mark (D152).
- `src/engine/fish.js`: A fish's weight is worked out, never stored (D146): `fishWeight` from its species' `lengthWeight` and its own `condition`, rolled from the `condition` stream wherever a fish is rolled. Two fish of a different condition don't stack (`sameFish`).
- `src/engine/fish.js`: The fish schema requires only id, name, size, difficulty and rarity, and the code falls back quietly when `swim`, `fights`, `taste` or `lengthWeight` is missing: a new fish wants them all.
- `src/engine/game.js`: Every item that comes to the player goes through `receive()` (D106).
- `src/engine/game.js`: `strikeLine` may return a cast in phase `fight` instead of `done` (D141); the next strike, at or after `fightAt`, answers it.
- `src/engine/game.js`: `timingFrom` must carry `fightMs`: it isn't a game value, and without it a manoeuvre never runs out (D141).
- `src/engine/game.js`: Fish Find by depth (D137, D148) is one d100, checked once per depth's bonus; with no bonus the checks are one and nothing more is drawn (a test checks).
- `src/engine/game.js`: A non-fish rolls twice (D125): treasure, then stuff against `stuffThreshold` with Stuff Find, then junk. White things live in the `stuff` lists, never in junk's.
- `src/engine/game.js`: The fish that bites is mostly a pool fish (D157), taken out of the pool onto the line (`take` in `rollLine`) and put back if it gets away: strip `plain` and its pool fields (`n`, `x`, `y`, `since`, `stays`, `of`) before it reaches the bucket, as `catchFish` does.
- `src/engine/game.js`: The aquarium's meals happen on coming into the room (D152), in `travel`, from the `tank` stream, never while the player watches; an event's `go:` into the room runs them too (`src/engine/events.js`).
- `src/engine/game.js`: The `taste` stream is drawn only for a fish with a parasite risk, and for a bowl with more than one kind to pick from (D138).
- `src/engine/tables.js`: The spots hold the fish, not the locations (D101): a location's `fish` list is empty on purpose.
